# ISL gloss sentences with English, used to train the gloss language model and as translator tests.
# Constructed by the team following the ISL rules in docs/isl_language.md (time first, predicate last,
# wh-sign last, negation last, FINISH = completed). HAVE A DEAF ISL SIGNER CHECK AND EXTEND THIS FILE.
# Format:  [YN] [NEG] GLOSS GLOSS ... | English      [YN] = raised brows (yes/no question), [NEG] = head shake
#
# --- greetings and social

SORRY | Sorry.
